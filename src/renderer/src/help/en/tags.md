## What are tags for?

They show what kind of work you do, across all projects. With tags like "Meeting", "Support" or "Development", the [report](reports.md) shows how much time went into meetings overall, no matter in which project.

## Why do I tag activities and not entries?

Because the kind of work belongs to the activity: a "Customer meeting" is a meeting every time. That way you don't choose anything while booking, and no entry is forgotten. Every entry automatically has the tags of its activity.

## How many tags can an activity have?

As many as fit. Just keep in mind: its time then counts fully for each tag. The sums of the tags therefore add up to more than the total, see [reports](reports.md).

## Should I create customers as tags?

No, that's what [clients](clients.md) are for. A tag says what kind of work it was, not who it was for. Client sums add up; tag sums don't.

## What happens when I delete a tag?

It disappears from all activities and thus from past reports too. The tracked time itself stays unchanged.
