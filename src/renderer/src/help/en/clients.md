## What is a client?

Who you work for, for example a customer or an internal department. Usually it's the question of whom the time is billed to.

## Why can't I choose one when booking?

Because it follows from the activity. This keeps booking fast, and the same work doesn't end up with one customer one day and another the next.

## Does it belong on the project or on the activity?

Usually on the project. If a whole project works for one customer, the client then applies to all activities in it. If a project works for several, for example a product with client-specific customizing, each activity gets its own.

## Why can't I choose one on an activity?

Because its project already sets one. The project always takes precedence. That way an activity never has two clients.

## What happens when I change it on the project?

- **Setting it**: It applies to all activities of the project and replaces their own.
- **Removing it**: The activities keep it individually. Nothing is lost.

Both apply retroactively to all time. Taktra warns you before a different client is replaced.

## And when I move an activity?

It keeps its former client, unless the new project sets its own.

## Can I rely on the sums per client?

Yes. Every minute counts for at most one client, and the sums add up to the total. Tags are different, see [reports](#/help/reports).
