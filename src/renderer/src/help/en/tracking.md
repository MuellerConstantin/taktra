## Timer or adding time afterwards, which is better?

Neither. Both create an ordinary entry, and reports treat them the same. Use the timer when you track while you work, and add what you forgot afterwards.

## Why does only one timer run?

Because you only work on one thing at a time. When you start something new, the old timer stops automatically. This also applies across profiles.

## Why does it say 1 minute when the timer only ran for 10 seconds?

Taktra books whole minutes. Start and end are rounded to the minute, and at least one minute is booked. In return, two timers in a row meet without a gap.

## How do I start a timer without opening Taktra?

With the quick start: press the [shortcut](#/settings/controls), search an activity, press Enter.

## New activity or note?

Usually a note. "Weekly sync" is an activity; this week's topic goes into the note of the entry. Otherwise your report falls apart into lots of single appointments. Notes also go into the [export](#/export) unchanged.

## Where do I see what I forgot?

In [time tracking](#/tracking). Gaps between two entries are shown there as untracked time.
