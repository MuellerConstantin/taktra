## When do I create a new activity?

Only for a new kind of work. Book recurring work on the same activity every time: one "Weekly sync" with many entries, not a "Weekly sync March 12" each week. Details of the single appointment go into the note.

Activities are created right while booking. When you type a name, Taktra suggests matching existing activities, including those of other projects.

## I created an activity twice. What now?

Merge them. All entries move to the other activity, and the duplicate disappears. The entries take over the project, tags and client of the activity they end up in.

## The activity is in the wrong project.

Move it. All of its time goes along to the new project, including that of past months.

## Why does my old report suddenly look different?

Because you changed an activity. Tags and [clients](#/help/clients) belong to the activity, not to the entry. Every change therefore applies to all of its time, retroactively too. This is intended: you can improve a classification later without touching every entry.

## How do I delete an activity?

You don't, you archive it. It then no longer shows up when you start a timer, but its time stays in the report. If you delete all of its entries, it disappears automatically.
