## Why is the sum of the tags larger than the total?

Because an activity can have several tags, and its time counts fully for each of them. Two hours tagged "Meeting" and "Support" show up with two hours under both. Added up, that's four, but two were worked.

This is not an error. It answers the question "how much time had to do with meetings?" correctly. Tags just don't make a total.

## Which sums do add up?

Projects and [clients](#/help/clients). Every activity belongs to exactly one project and has at most one client. Every minute therefore counts exactly once there.

## Why does an old report look different today?

Because an activity was changed since then, for example a new tag or another project. Such changes apply to all of its time, see [activities](#/help/activities).

## How do I keep a fixed state, for example for an invoice?

With an [export](#/export). The file stays as it is, even if you reorganize things later.

## Does archived time still count?

Yes. Archiving only tidies up; it doesn't change any tracked time.
