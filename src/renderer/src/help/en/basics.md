## The four levels

Taktra organizes your time in four levels. Each one sits inside the one above:

- **Profile**: A file with everything in it, for example "Work".
  - **Project**: What you work on, for example "Website relaunch".
    - **Activity**: What exactly you do, for example "Customer meeting".
      - **Entry**: When and how long, for example "Monday, 10:00–11:30".

You always book time on an activity. So every entry belongs to exactly one activity, and every activity to exactly one project.

## Tags and clients

Two more details classify your time. You set both on the activity, not on single entries:

- **Tag**: What kind of work, for example "Meeting" or "Support". An activity can have several.
- **Client**: Who it is for, for example a customer or a department. An activity has at most one.

## The most important rule

Whatever you change on an activity applies to all of its entries, old ones included. If it gets a new tag, all of its time counts for that tag. Almost everything surprising in Taktra follows from this rule.
